### Daniel Dizon

Daniel Dizon is the founder of [Rejoice](https://rejoice.global) and a founding contributor to [Always Foundation](https://alwaysfoundation.org). Since coming to faith in the Lord Jesus Christ at Easter 2025, he has built Christian technology and sovereign AI: private, privacy-preserving systems that people and churches own and control. A systems and protocol architect trained in information systems, he previously advised governments in Australia and the UK, and founded machine learning SaaS start-ups and the digital assets protocol Swell, before moving on in mid-2025 to build for the Church.

**Founder · Investor · Advisor**

Investments include Church AI, Firmus, Ethena, Derive and RedStone.

**Where I build**

- **[Rejoice](https://rejoice.global)** is a Christian technology company building apps for churches, creators and families. It builds on Always under the same open licence and terms as every other builder.
- **[Always Foundation](https://alwaysfoundation.org)**, where I'm a founding contributor, is a separate 501(c)(3) nonprofit. In its own words: *"Free, open, shared evangelism technology that connects churches and ministries, so no one who says yes to Jesus walks alone."* Its code is open source under Apache 2.0. Repos: [always-platform](https://github.com/alwaysfoundation/always-platform), [always-website](https://github.com/alwaysfoundation/always-website).

**Areas of interest**

- **Christian faith and flourishing technology.** Software for churches, ministries and Bible engagement, built to be trustworthy with people's data and faithful to its purpose.
- **Frontier technology and sovereign AI.** Private, self-hosted AI systems on open models that people, churches and communities can run, inspect and control: privacy-preserving, permissionless and censorship-resistant.
- **Advancing the gospel through frontier technology.** Putting the best of new technology to work carrying the true gospel of Jesus Christ further, to every nation and language.

**How I build**

- **Systems architecture.** Distributed systems and protocols.
- **Incentive design.** Mechanism design that rewards the behaviour a system needs.
- **Adversarial engineering.** Trust-minimised design that assumes some participants will misbehave.
- **Anti-fragility.** Systems that get stronger under stress rather than breaking.

**How I work in the open**

- Small, reviewed pull requests over large unreviewed drops.
- Tests with every change, and honest changelogs.
- AI-assisted work is disclosed in the PR, and I stand behind every line I submit.
